______________________________
   Chapter Name: If I Were You
   Author: Douglas James
______________________________


Characters:
___________________
Gerrard – a calm, intelligent playwright who lives alone in a secluded cottage
Intruder – a criminal and jewel thief who plans to kill Gerrard and assume his identity
Sergeant – a police officer mentioned at the end of the play
__________________________________________________________________________________


Short Summary:
___________________
The chapter is a one-act play that revolves around a tense encounter between Gerrard and an armed intruder. Gerrard lives alone in a quiet cottage when a criminal breaks in, intending to kill him and take his identity to escape the police. The intruder believes Gerrard is an ideal victim because he lives alone, has few visitors, and moves around secretly. Instead of panicking, Gerrard remains calm and uses his intelligence, wit, and presence of mind to manipulate the intruder psychologically. He tells the intruder that he himself is a criminal constantly on the run and that killing him would only complicate matters for the intruder. Confused and frightened, the intruder lowers his guard, allowing Gerrard to push him into a cupboard and lock him inside. Gerrard then calls the police, successfully outwitting the criminal. The play highlights the power of intelligence and calm thinking over physical force.
