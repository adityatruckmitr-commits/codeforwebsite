   Chapter Name: Kathmandu   
   Author: Vikram Seth       
_____________________________


Characters:
___________________
Vikram Seth – the narrator and traveller
Mr Shah’s son and nephew – companions during the visit
Priests – performing rituals at Pashupatinath temple
Devotees, hawkers, tourists – people present around the temples
Flute seller – a calm, meditative musician in the Kathmandu square
__________________________________________________________________________________


Short Summary:
___________________
The chapter is a travelogue describing the author’s visit to Kathmandu, the capital of Nepal. Vikram Seth visits two famous religious places—Pashupatinath temple and the Baudhnath stupa—and presents a vivid contrast between them. Pashupatinath is shown as a place of chaos and intense activity, filled with priests, devotees, animals, tourists, and rituals along the Bagmati river. In contrast, the Baudhnath stupa offers peace and stillness, surrounded by Tibetan shops and quiet devotion. The author also describes the lively streets of Kathmandu, full of shops, sounds, food, and everyday life. Towards the end, he is deeply moved by a flute seller whose music rises above the city’s noise and connects him emotionally to humanity and memories of home. The chapter highlights the blend of spirituality, culture, noise, calm, and human emotion in Kathmandu.

  Poem Name: A Slumber Did My Spirit Seal  
  Poet: William Wordsworth                 
___________________________________________


Summary:
___________________
The poem reflects the poet’s feelings after the death of a loved one. Initially, he feels a deep calm and acceptance rather than fear or sorrow. He realises that time and worldly suffering can no longer affect her. In the final lines, the poet imagines her as a part of nature, merged with the earth, rocks, stones, and trees. The poem expresses quiet acceptance of death and the idea of unity between human life and nature.
__________________________________________________________________________________



Literary Instruments:
___________________
Personification – abstract ideas like time and spirit are given human qualities
Imagery – images of earth, rocks, stones, and trees
Metaphor – death is presented as a peaceful sleep
Alliteration – repetition of consonant sounds
Symbolism – nature symbolises eternity and permanence
