   Chapter Name: A Truly Beautiful Mind
   Author: NCERT (Life of Albert Einstein)
______________________________


Characters:
___________________
Albert Einstein – a brilliant scientist and central figure of the chapter
Einstein’s Mother – initially worried about Albert’s development and opposed his marriage
Mileva Maric – Einstein’s fellow student, wife, and intellectual companion
Elsa Einstein – Einstein’s cousin and second wife
Franklin D. Roosevelt – President of the United States to whom Einstein wrote a warning letter
American Physicists – scientists concerned about nuclear weapons during World War II
__________________________________________________________________________________


Short Summary:
___________________
The chapter presents a detailed biographical account of Albert Einstein, tracing his journey from childhood to becoming one of the greatest scientific minds in history. As a child, Einstein was considered slow and unusual, but he showed deep curiosity and originality of thought. He disliked strict schooling and preferred independent thinking, which led him to leave school in Munich and continue his studies in Switzerland. While working as a clerk in a patent office, he developed revolutionary scientific ideas, including the Special Theory of Relativity and the famous equation E = mc². His later work on the General Theory of Relativity brought him worldwide fame and the Nobel Prize. The chapter also highlights Einstein’s personal struggles, marriages, political concerns, and his role as a peace-loving world citizen who warned against the misuse of nuclear weapons.

  Poem Name: The Lake Isle of Innisfree
  Poet: W. B. Yeats
____________________________________________


Summary:
___________________
The poem expresses the poet’s deep longing for peace and solitude in a quiet natural place called Innisfree. The poet dreams of leaving the noisy city life and living alone in a small cabin surrounded by nature, bees, birds, and the lake. The sights and sounds of Innisfree bring him inner peace and calm. Even when he is far away, standing on busy roads, the sound of the lake echoes in his heart. The poem suggests that Innisfree represents both a real place and a peaceful state of mind that the poet deeply yearns for.
__________________________________________________________________________________


Literary Instruments:
___________________
Imagery – vivid pictures of nature, lake, birds, and peaceful surroundings
Repetition – repeated use of “I will arise and go now” to express longing
Alliteration – repetition of consonant sounds
Metaphor – Innisfree represents peace and inner harmony
Symbolism – the lake and natural sounds symbolise calmness and spiritual peace
