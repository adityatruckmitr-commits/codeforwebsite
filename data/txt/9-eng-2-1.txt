Chapter Name: The Lost Child
Author: Mulk Raj Anand
Characters: The child, Mother, Father, Fair-goers, Stranger
Short Summary: A young child visits a spring fair with his parents and is fascinated by toys, sweets, and rides. When he gets separated from his parents in the crowd, all material desires lose meaning and he longs only for his mother and father, showing that parental love is the greatest need.
