______________________________
   Chapter Name: The Little Girl
   Author: Katherine Mansfield
______________________________


Characters:
___________________
Kezia – a little girl who is afraid of her strict father
Father – Kezia’s strict, hardworking, and reserved father
Mother – Kezia’s mother who follows the father’s discipline
Grandmother – a loving and understanding figure in Kezia’s life
Mr Macdonald – Kezia’s neighbour, a cheerful and playful father
Alice – the cook who looks after Kezia when her mother is ill 
__________________________________________________________________________________


Short Summary:
___________________
The story describes the changing relationship between a little girl named Kezia and her father. Initially, Kezia is terrified of her father because of his strict nature, loud voice, and lack of affection. She misunderstands his seriousness as cruelty and feels relieved when he leaves for work. When Kezia unknowingly tears her father’s important papers to make a birthday gift, she is punished harshly, which deepens her fear. By observing Mr Macdonald, a playful and affectionate neighbour, Kezia believes that there are different kinds of fathers. One night, when Kezia is alone and frightened by a nightmare, her father comforts her tenderly and lets her sleep beside him. This experience helps Kezia realise that her father is loving at heart but unable to express affection openly. She finally understands him as a caring human being.

  Poem Name: Rain on the Roof
  Poet: Coates Kinney
____________________________________________


Summary:
___________________
The poem describes the poet’s feelings while listening to the soft patter of rain falling on the roof at night. The sound of rain creates a calm and comforting atmosphere that fills the poet with happiness and peace. As he listens, his mind becomes filled with memories and dreamy thoughts, especially memories of his mother lovingly watching over him in childhood. The rain becomes a source of emotional connection, bringing warmth, nostalgia, and a sense of security. The poem highlights the soothing power of nature and the deep bond between a mother and her child.
__________________________________________________________________________________


Literary Instruments:
___________________
Imagery – vivid pictures of rain, dark clouds, and a cosy room
Alliteration – repetition of consonant sounds
Personification – rain is described as gently weeping
Repetition – repeated reference to the sound of rain
Metaphor – rain symbolises comfort, memory, and emotional warmth
