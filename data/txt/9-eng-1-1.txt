______________________________
   Chapter Name: The Fun They Had
   Author: Isaac Asimov
______________________________


Characters:
___________________
Margie – an eleven-year-old girl who studies at home with a mechanical teacher  
Tommy – a thirteen-year-old boy who finds an old printed book  
Mrs Jones – Margie’s mother who supervises her education  
County Inspector – repairs and adjusts Margie’s mechanical teacher  
__________________________________________________________________________________


Short Summary:
___________________
The story is set in the year 2157, a future where books and schools no longer exist in their traditional form. Children study at home using mechanical teachers and telebooks displayed on screens. Margie and her friend Tommy discover an old printed book that describes schools of the past, where children learned together in classrooms under human teachers. Margie dislikes her mechanical teacher because it gives her frequent tests and teaches lessons too fast. The County Inspector repairs the machine and adjusts its speed. As Margie reads about old schools, she imagines children studying, playing, and learning together. While attending her lesson, Margie feels sad and nostalgic, thinking about how enjoyable the old schools must have been and reflects on “the fun they had."

  Poem Name: The Road Not Taken
  Poet: Robert Frost
____________________________________________


Summary:
___________________
The poem describes a traveller standing at a point where two roads diverge in a forest and facing the difficulty of choosing between them. He chooses the road that appears less travelled, knowing that this decision will shape his future. The poet reflects on how choices are inevitable in life and how each decision leads to different outcomes. The poem suggests that choices once made cannot easily be undone and that they play a crucial role in defining a person’s life.
__________________________________________________________________________________


Literary Instruments:
___________________
Metaphor – the roads represent choices in life
Symbolism – the diverging paths symbolise life decisions
Imagery – vivid description of the forest and roads
Rhyme scheme – ABAAB
